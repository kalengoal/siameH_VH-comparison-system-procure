How to Use the Procurement System

Simple guide: Work from the hospital list, add the suppliers, enter or upload their quotations, check the comparison, then finalize and produce the reports.

Step 1 — Start a procurement

Click New Procurement. Enter the reference, description and date. Then click Save when you want to keep your work.

Step 2 — Prepare the pharmacist's list

The pharmacist creates the master list using Item Name, Size and Quantity. You can type the items one by one or use Import Excel/CSV. This is the hospital's requested list.

Step 3 — Add the suppliers

Enter the names of the suppliers who are quoting. Start with the suppliers you have and use + Add Supplier when you need more. The system supports 20+ suppliers.

Step 4 — Choose how to enter quotations

A — Manual: Use the manual price-entry table when you want to type the supplier's quotation yourself. Enter the quoted pack size and unit price for the correct supplier.

B — Bulk upload: Upload supplier quotations in PDF, JPG, JPEG or PNG format. The system tries to read and match the quotation. Always review the extracted information before applying it.

Step 5 — If the supplier uses a different pack size

Do not reject it automatically. Enter the supplier's actual quoted pack size. The system keeps the hospital's requested size unchanged and clearly flags the different pack size so you can make the decision.

Step 6 — Enter prices quickly

When entering prices manually, use the keyboard Tab key. Tab moves down the same supplier column to the next medicine. After the last medicine, it moves to the first medicine for the next supplier. Shift + Tab moves backwards.

Step 7 — Work with large lists

You do not need to scroll through 1,000 medicines at once. Use First, Previous, Next and Last to move between pages. You can choose 10, 20, 50 or 100 items per page.

Step 8 — Compare prices

Click Compare Prices after the supplier quotations have been entered. Review the results carefully. The system identifies the lowest available quoted price and flags important differences for your decision.

Step 9 — Finalize

When you are satisfied with the comparison and supplier selections, click Finalize Comparison. Review before confirming.

Step 10 — Produce the reports

Use Print Supplier Reports (A4) for printable supplier documents and Save Excel Reports for the Excel reports. The Supplier Supply Lists belong in the reports, not on the main working screen.

Step 11 — Continue later

Click Save before closing. Later, open the procurement from Saved Procurements. Your entered prices and quotation information should remain available.

What to do if a quotation upload fails

Do not stop the procurement. Use A — Manual Supplier Price Entry and enter the supplier's quotation manually. Bulk upload is optional; manual entry remains available.

Important rule

The supplier's quotation format is not controlled by the hospital. The system therefore allows different supplier formats and pack sizes, while keeping the pharmacist's master list as the reference.

About

Victoria Hospital Procurement Comparison System

A standalone procurement comparison tool for preparing a hospital master list, recording supplier quotations, comparing prices and producing supplier supply lists.

Workflow: Pharmacist master list → supplier quotations → manual A or bulk B → review → comparison.
